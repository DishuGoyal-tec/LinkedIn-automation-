Chaayos next-cafe location (SQL) | I built a SQL project in MySQL that asks where Chaayos should open its next cafe in Gurugram. Problem: a wrong spot either misses footfall or steals sales from the chain's own nearby outlet. Data: Google Maps data that I collected and cleaned, with 34 existing Chaayos outlets, 223 demand points (malls, office parks, hospitals, colleges, metro stations), 15 direct competitors and 56 premium cafes. Method: I scored 60 candidate sites (malls and office parks) within a 1 km radius, using demand from nearby places weighted by Google review counts, a penalty for nearby Chaayos outlets and a penalty for direct competitors; a site within 0.5 km of an existing outlet was dropped. SQL used: joins, CTEs, window functions, correlated subqueries and spatial distance with ST_Distance_Sphere. Result: top 5 areas were Sahara Mall, Orchid Business Park, Sewa Tower, Baani Square and DLF World Techpark. I kept a cleaning log of 200+ removed rows with a reason for each. Honest limits: the score partly agrees with existing outlets (Spearman 0.51 with outlet review counts) but not fully, the top picks changed with the search radius, review count is only a proxy for footfall, and distances are straight line. So the result is a shortlist for site visits, not a final answer.
Pizza sales dashboard (Power BI) | I built a two-page Power BI dashboard for a pizza business: a Business Overview page with KPIs and trends, and a Product Analysis page with best and worst sellers, top 10 and bottom 10 pizzas by revenue and category contribution. KPIs: total revenue 68.23K, 1,773 orders, 4,107 pizzas sold, average order value 38.48. Insights: revenue is mostly driven by the Classic and Supreme categories, a small group of pizzas contributes a large share of revenue, and sales patterns change over time. Recommendations: promote top pizzas, use offers or combos for weak categories, plan staffing and inventory from demand trends, and upsell to raise average order value. Tools: Power Query, data modelling, DAX measures, interactive filters for category, size and month.
Uber trip dashboard (Excel) | I built an interactive Excel dashboard on Uber trip data using pivot tables, pivot charts, slicers, GETPIVOTDATA and KPI cards, so every visual updates when a slicer changes. It covers a city filter, day versus night shift, payment type contribution, vehicle-wise performance, monthly booking trends, time-bin demand and top pickup locations. Insights: most bookings happen in the day shift, a few vehicle categories bring much more trip value, evening time bins show strong activity, digital payments dominate but cash still matters, and a few pickup points account for a large share of trips. What I learned: designing dashboards for non-technical stakeholders and building connected pivot systems controlled by slicers.
Ugaoo sales EDA (Python) | I did an exploratory data analysis in Python (pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) on e-commerce sales data of Ugaoo, a gardening brand. Questions: which states and categories drive profit, how payment mode and discounts affect profit, and where returns create risk. Findings: profit is concentrated in a few states (Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh contribute a large share), Flowering Plants and Outdoor Plants are the most profitable categories, Cash on Delivery has the largest share of orders but higher return tendency, higher discounts reduce average profit margin, and large discounts on high-value orders signal possible revenue leakage. Recommendations: expand what works in the strong regions, prioritise high-margin categories, encourage prepaid payment and control discounts.
Ugaoo analytics plus chatbot | I built a business analytics portfolio project around Ugaoo, a D2C gardening brand. It combines Power BI dashboards (a Customer Analysis dashboard and an Executive dashboard) with an AI chatbot that answers questions from company documents using RAG. Stack: Python, FastAPI, pandas, React with TypeScript, Ollama, FAISS and Docker.
